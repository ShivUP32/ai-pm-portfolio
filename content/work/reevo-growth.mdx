## The Challenge
At ADDA EDUCATION, launching **Reevo** required solving a core problem in ed-tech: high Day 1 drop-off during onboarding. Traditional generic onboarding flows failed to capture user context, leading to friction. We needed a deeply personalized, intelligent onboarding journey to drive initial engagement and long-term retention.

![Reevo Onboarding Dashboard](/images/reevo.png)
*Reevo's highly personalized, AI-driven onboarding dashboard*

## The Solution
We completely reimagined the onboarding experience by integrating a **Voice-First AI Assistant** directly into the sign-up flow.

### 1. Personalized Growth Journeys
Instead of static forms, users interacted with an AI agent that dynamically adjusted questions based on their responses. This created a custom learning path from minute one.

### 2. Multi-Agent Orchestration
Behind the scenes, we utilized a multi-agent framework:
- **Intake Agent:** Handled natural language understanding of the user's goals.
- **Curriculum Agent:** Dynamically generated the optimal course sequence.
- **Support Agent:** Proactively reached out if the user stalled during onboarding.

### 3. Voice-First Interaction
By allowing users to speak their goals rather than type them, we reduced onboarding friction significantly, especially for users accessing the platform via mobile in non-optimal typing environments.

## Results & Impact
The personalized AI onboarding wasn't just a gimmick—it was a massive growth lever.

- **150K+ Downloads:** Accelerated organic word-of-mouth growth.
- **10X Revenue Increase:** Driven by higher activation rates and personalized upselling at the end of the onboarding flow.
- **55%+ D1 Retention:** A massive leap over industry standards, proving that personalized first impressions stick.
